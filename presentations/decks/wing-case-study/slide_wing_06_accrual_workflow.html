Module 2 — PO Accrual Automation

Automated Workflow

1

Auto-populate

Month-end accrual population pulled straight from the GR/IR aging report (MB5S), not a manual PO download.

2

Auto-post

Recurring JE template posts the accrual; manual review reserved only for balances above a set $ threshold.

3

Reverse & net

Auto-reversing entry clears Day 1 of the new month; the actual invoice nets against GR/IR with no manual tracking.

4

Accrual-to-actual

Monthly variance by PO/vendor, tolerance-based escalation, tracked as a rolling accrual-accuracy KPI.
