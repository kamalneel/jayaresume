Module 1 — Prepaid Policy

Below Threshold & Audit Support

Immediate Expensing

  • Full expense at invoice/payment to the natural GL line, regardless of service period.
  • Exception: recurring multi-period items (e.g. annual insurance) still amortized for trend consistency.
  • Quarterly look-back on the population of sub-threshold recurring spend, to evidence aggregate immateriality.

Audit Documentation

  • Source invoice or contract with explicit service-period dates.
  • Cost-center-owner sign-off on nature and period of benefit.
  • System-generated amortization schedule — not a spreadsheet.
  • Second-tier approval (Controller) above $100K.
